Privacy and analytics

This technical portfolio uses optional Google Analytics 4 after a visitor chooses “allow analytics”. The site remains usable when analytics is declined.

What is measured

Google Analytics may record the visited page, landing page, referrer or traffic channel, device and browser class, session activity, and approximate country or city. The site does not intentionally send names, email addresses, CV contents, or form-entered personal information to Analytics.

Advertising

Advertising storage, advertising user data, personalization signals, and Google Signals are disabled by the site configuration. Analytics is used to understand aggregate readership and page usage, not to build advertising profiles.

Consent

Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage under atlas.analytics.consent.v1. Declining prevents the Google Analytics tag from loading. You can reset the choice below and select again.

Google documentation

Google describes its Analytics safeguards and regional data handling in its data safeguards documentation. Google states that IP addresses are used for coarse geolocation and discarded before being logged.

Contact

Questions about this site's analytics can be sent to pavel.zosim@protonmail.com.

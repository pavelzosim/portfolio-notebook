Editorial draft. Screenshots are explicit placeholders. This record covers the implemented geometry and shader pipeline. Building-scale glass checks and fabric-color integration are still in progress.

01 A module is more than its mesh

The procedural environment system in 9 PASSAGE places walls, openings and other structural pieces according to authored rules. Controlled Procedurality explains that placement system. This record looks at the assets it places: the concrete facade panel, its window, glass and curtain, and the data that lets those parts vary together.

A panel can repeat structurally without needing to repeat visually. I wanted to keep a reusable module while varying chipped edges, dirt, water marks and the state of the window. Making a separate complete mesh-and-texture set for every combination would tie those choices together too early. Instead, I separated the physical damage variants from the surface and window controls.

The asset pipeline combines Houdini geometry and attributes, Substance Designer and Painter material authoring, and Unreal material functions. VDB changes the wall surface and damaged silhouette. Vertex data carries spatial masks into the material. Vellum provides the curtain geometry; a vertex shader changes how that existing geometry is gathered at the window.

The distinction matters at export: a material mask cannot repair a missing silhouette, and a damaged mesh does not automatically describe the roughness or exposed surface of its chips. Those are related outputs with different jobs. The working branches are Clean LQ and Medium/Heavy damage variants. Their runtime LOD policy is outside the scope of this breakdown.

02 Give the geometry useful data

The starting panel is a volume with a window opening and additional joint contours. Its useful edges are not limited to the outer rectangle: the reveal around the window and the panel joints also need to participate in damage. A rectangular UV border would not describe those features.

I use SDF/VDB processing to introduce surface irregularity and to prepare geometry-related masks. For edge classification, the implementation uses Labs Measure Curvature with the Tensor - Integral method, followed by wrangle processing and blur. Convex and concave information are kept distinct: exposed edges and recesses are different spatial signals, even when both eventually contribute to aging.

The candidate damage mask is formed on simplified geometry. The purpose is control over the larger edge structure rather than allowing every small surface fluctuation on a dense mesh to become a damage candidate. The dense branch still supplies geometric detail; it does not have to be the best domain on which to author the broad selection.

Transfer by surface position, not point number

Reduction and VDB processing change topology, so a point index is not a reliable correspondence between the branches. The transfer uses xyzdist() to locate the closest primitive and its parametric coordinates, then primuv() to interpolate the attribute at that location. These are primitive coordinates used for surface sampling; they are not the exported texture UV set.

The following is a reconstruction of that transfer pattern, not a verbatim export of the project wrangle. Input 0 is the destination, input 1 is the reduced mask source, and both must be in the same space.

// VEX / Point Wrangle — illustrative nearest-surface mask transfer
int source_prim = -1;
vector source_uvw = 0;
float distance = xyzdist(1, @P, source_prim, source_uvw);

if (source_prim >= 0 && distance <= chf("max_transfer_distance"))
{
    float mask = primuv(1, "edge_damage_candidate",
                       source_prim, source_uvw);
    f@edge_damage_candidate = clamp(mask, 0.0, 1.0);
}

Nearest-surface transfer still needs inspection around closely spaced faces and the window reveal. A nearby surface is not necessarily the intended corresponding surface. The distance guard above is an illustrative safeguard; the actual grouping and transfer limits need to be checked against the project network before turning this into a reproducible node-by-node recipe.

Labs Edge Damage in VDB mode supplies the geometric chips. Medium and Heavy variants retain different amounts of physical damage. The material then reads a damage region rather than trying to infer every chip from the rendered normal. Exact mesh detail and interpolated mask detail need not coincide point for point.

Treat channels as a local interface

On the concrete mesh, the chip mask uses Cd.B; existing R and G data are preserved. The wall material selects a color component through a dot product with ChannelSelector, so the material function is not permanently wired to B.

The glass uses G for another purpose: distinguishing its two panes. These are mesh-local contracts, not a universal meaning assigned to green or blue across the whole project. An asset must preserve the channel meanings expected by its consuming material. Vertex interpolation also limits the spatial detail a mask can represent; adding a channel is not equivalent to adding a full-resolution mask texture.

03 Build aging over the authored surface

Substance Designer and Painter belong to the surface-authoring side of this pipeline; Unreal does not replace that authored base with random color. MF_SurfaceSample supplies Base Color, Normal and ORMH inputs to M_Master_Wall. The precise ORMH channel layout must remain the one used by that function; this draft does not assume a packing order from its name.

Over this base, the wall material separates bottom dirt, leaks and chips into independently controllable operations. That separation lets a panel retain the same concrete character while its exposure and damage state change.

Dirt and leaks describe different regions

MF_Panel_BottomDirt controls a lower band with its own height, transition softness, breakup and roughness. Noise changes the boundary of the band instead of making the whole surface uniformly dirty.

MF_Panel_LeakStreaks has two passes: one associated with the window and another originating at the top panel boundary. Their positions, widths, start/end coordinates and softness are independent. Instance values can change their length, intensity, offset and probability. Sharing one pass for both origins would couple effects that need different placement.

A chip changes a surface, not just its brightness

MF_Panel_EdgeWear combines the mesh damage signal with noise breakup and an instance-dependent UV offset. MF_Panel_ChipSurface then changes Base Color, Roughness and Normal in the selected region. The chip is therefore treated as a distinct surface response rather than only a dark outline.

The geometric branch controls the actual edge silhouette. The shader adds variation within the exported damage region; it does not claim to generate a new physical chip in every HISM instance. Two instances of the same Medium mesh still share that geometric silhouette.

04 Glass needs its own coordinate and layer model

The window contains two physically separated glass layers. Both use M_Master_Glass, while a saturated VertexColor.G selector distinguishes their parameters. Roughness, transmittance and aging intensity can differ between the outer and inner pane without requiring a separate master material.

The procedural window can contain a varying number of glass elements. I keep the primary texture layout in UV0 and use a normalized local layout in UV1 for edge distance, dust and streak placement. This gives each glass element a usable local domain even when its main texture islands are arranged differently.

The current glass setup uses Thin Translucent, a thin surface, Surface ForwardShading and no refraction. The project blend setting is TranslucentGreyTransmittance. I will include the settings capture with the glass breakdown; these choices describe this project setup.

The glass functions include MF_Glass_EdgeDust, MF_Glass_DustDistribution and MF_Glass_DustBreakup for where dust accumulates; MF_Glass_DustTransmittance for its effect on transmission; and separate MicroRoughness, ScratchMask, WaterStreaks and MineralTransmittance functions.

For water marks, an instance-derived scalar perturbs the effective end coordinate. The strip shapes still come from the streak texture. Mineral deposits then attenuate the already dust-adjusted transmittance:

// HLSL equivalent of the reported mineral-transmittance composition.
// MineralMask is already available; this block adds no texture sample.
float3 FinalTransmittance = saturate(
    BaseTransmittance * lerp(1.0, MineralTransmittance,
                            MineralMask * Strength));

Two thin surfaces do not constitute a volumetric simulation of an insulating glass unit. The system is an approximation for real-time rendering. The graph is assembled. The remaining building-scale checks are transparency ordering, reflections, two-layer interaction and Shader Complexity on the complete facade.

05 Author the folds once, control the state in the shader

The curtain mesh is authored with Vellum in Houdini and retains actual folds in its geometry. That is the authoring stage. Runtime gathering uses WPO on the exported mesh; it is not a claim that Unreal is running the original Vellum simulation.

One curtain supports three configurations: closed across the opening, gathered at the left, or gathered at the right. An additional UV channel supplies a normalized horizontal coordinate u. A width scale s and an anchor a define the local horizontal offset.

// Local-space curtain gathering model from the implementation notes.
// u: normalized horizontal coordinate; Width: original local width.
// Anchor: 0 for left, 1 for right; Scale: 1 keeps the full width.
float GatherOffset = (Scale - 1.0) * (u - Anchor) * Width;
float FoldOffset = sin(2.0 * PI * FoldCount * u)
                 * FoldAmplitude * (1.0 - Scale);
float3 OffsetLocal = WidthAxisLocal * GatherOffset
                   + DepthAxisLocal * FoldOffset;
// Transform OffsetLocal as a vector from Local to World, then output WPO.

At an anchor of zero, the left edge stays fixed; at one, the right edge stays fixed. At a scale of one, both offsets vanish. The implementation uses a full-width closed state and gathered scales in the 0.18–0.35 range.

Simple width compression alone makes the folded surface appear flattened. The second offset adds depth variation along the curtain's local depth axis as gathering increases. Both terms are combined in local space before vector conversion to world space. This is a geometric correction for the gathered look, not a fresh physically solved cloth state.

Reuse the gathering controls for fabric density

MF_Curtain_WPO exposes CurtainCompression and CurtainFoldDensity. The control signals are:

// Control signals reported by MF_Curtain_WPO.
float Compression = saturate((1.0 - Scale) / 0.8);
float FoldDensity = Compression * abs(cos(2.0 * PI * FoldCount * u));

These signals describe the same gathering operation that moves the mesh. A fabric-density treatment can therefore follow the compressed state instead of receiving an unrelated noise mask. For the current Masked fabric, the next step is to connect these signals to Dither Opacity Mask as an approximation of partial transparency.

Tint fabric without replacing its texture

MF_Curtain_RandomColor varies hue and value in HSV, with saturation and tint strength controlled separately. The tint is applied over the authored fabric texture so that its surface detail is retained. The function logic is implemented; connecting it to the final facade material is still in progress.

06 Repeat the module without coupling every decision

HISM instances share assets while material-level choices change leaks, texture offsets and window states. MF_InstanceRandomSelector supplies continuous, binary, discrete and balanced-discrete modes. Curtain state uses a discrete choice; gathering width uses a continuous range. The balanced mode requires a valid instance index and serves a different purpose from an independent random draw.

Those modes are already discussed in The Light System, together with the custom interior cubemap. Here the boundary is important: the cubemap supplies the apparent room behind a window, the glass modifies the surface through which it is seen, and the curtain changes the foreground opening. Reusing one random value for all three would couple distinct visual decisions.

A fixed seed makes a given input mapping repeatable. It does not by itself make a cosmetic PerInstanceRandom or instance-index choice stable across a reordered or rebuilt HISM population. Placement identity and light synchronization remain responsibilities of the environment system described in the linked records.

07 Result, limits and remaining validation

The current pipeline produces Medium/Heavy Houdini damage variants and an integrated wall-aging material on the facade. The glass graph, UV1 layout and pane selector are assembled. Curtain gathering and the fold correction are implemented; fabric color and the proposed opacity treatment have separate integration status.

The reusable part of this work is the interface between stages. A geometry mask survives as mesh data, a material interprets it as a surface change, and a small set of state controls can affect more than one visual property. Those links make variation coherent. They do not remove the cost of WPO, masked coverage, translucent overdraw or complex material sampling.

The next validation pass will compare the same facade with each group enabled and disabled, inspect exported channels after mesh processing, and profile the complete window stack at representative density.

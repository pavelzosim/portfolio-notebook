FX Flame simulation vertex animation

FX flame simulation vertex animation using Houdini VAT workflow for Unreal Engine and Unity real-time pipelines.

Watch as I showcase key steps, including geometry preparation, loop animation, shader creation, and particle system building for sparks, hot air convection, and burnout sticks.
